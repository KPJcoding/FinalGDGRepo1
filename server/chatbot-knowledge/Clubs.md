3. KSHITIJ 2024
Kshitij 2024, held on January 19th and 20th, witnessed an epic clash between the Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) departments.
The event featured numerous sports, namely:
Cricket, Football, Kabaddi, Basketball, Volleyball, Tug of War, Carrom, Table Tennis, Shot Put, 100m Sprint, Relay, and Badminton.

6. WORLD BICYCLE DAY
The Indian Institute of Information Technology, Nagpur organized a cycle rally on the occasion of World Bicycle Day, which is celebrated annually on June 3rd and recognizes the power and versatility of the bicycle.
The institute provided bicycles and refreshments to the participants. The event commenced at 5:45 AM, with the Registrar giving the green flag as the sun rose, marking the beginning of the rally.
The rally began with great enthusiasm and fervor as participants embarked on the designated route, covering a distance of 5 km. The route passed through Waranga village, allowing participants to engage with the local surroundings.
Throughout the rally, participants displayed remarkable enthusiasm and support for the cause by waving banners and spreading the message of sustainable transportation. The event witnessed the participation of 60 participants and volunteers from various age groups and backgrounds, all coming together to advocate for a greener and healthier future.
The event was graced by the esteemed presence of the Registrar of IIIT Nagpur, Shri Kailas N. Dakhale. The rally concluded at the Institute gate, where a closing ceremony was held to appreciate the participants’ efforts and dedication.
The cycle rally proved to be a resounding success, receiving an overwhelming response from the community and reinforcing the importance of eco-friendly transportation.

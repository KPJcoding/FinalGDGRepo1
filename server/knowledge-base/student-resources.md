## Scholarships and Financial Aid

### Merit Scholarships

**Institute Merit Scholarship**
- Eligibility: CGPA > 9.0
- Amount: Full tuition waiver
- Renewable: Yes, if CGPA maintained

**Academic Excellence Award**
- Eligibility: Top 10% of batch
- Amount: ₹50,000 per semester
- Application: Automatic (no application needed)

### Need-Based Scholarships

**Financial Assistance Program**
- Eligibility: Family income < ₹5 lakhs/year
- Amount: Up to 100% tuition fee waiver
- Application: Submit income certificate in July

### Government Scholarships

Students can apply for:
- Central Sector Scholarship Scheme
- Post Matric Scholarship
- National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

**Scholarship Office:** scholarships@iiitn.ac.in

- Capacity: 500 students
- Room Type: Double occupancy (2 students per room)
- Facilities: Wi-Fi, common room, study area, mess

**Girls Hostel**
- Capacity: 300 students

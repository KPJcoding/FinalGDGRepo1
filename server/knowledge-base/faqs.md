# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## General Questions

### What is Sol-1?
Sol-1 is IIIT Nagpur's internal knowledge hub platform where students can ask academic questions, share solutions, and build a permanent knowledge base for future batches.

### Who can access Sol-1?
Only IIIT Nagpur students with valid @iiitn.ac.in email addresses can access the platform.

### Is Sol-1 free to use?
Yes, Sol-1 is completely free for all IIIT Nagpur students.

## Account and Authentication

### How do I sign up?
1. Click "Get Started" on the homepage
2. Enter your @iiitn.ac.in email
3. Verify the OTP sent to your email
4. Complete your profile with name, batch, and branch
5. Start using the platform

### I didn't receive the OTP email. What should I do?
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Ensure you entered the correct email address
- Wait 5 minutes before requesting a new OTP
- Contact support if the issue persists

### Can I change my email address?
Currently, email addresses cannot be changed. Contact support if you need to update your email.

## Credits and Scoring

### How do I earn credits?
- Ask quality questions
- Provide helpful answers
- Get your answers accepted by question authors
- Achieve peer verification
- Get maintainer verification

### What are credit tiers?
Credit tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) are based on your total accumulated credits. Higher tiers unlock special privileges and recognition.

### Do credits expire?
No, credits are permanent and carry forward throughout your time at IIIT Nagpur.

## Questions and Answers

### Can I edit my question after posting?
Currently, questions cannot be edited. You can post a new question or add clarifications in comments.

### Can I delete my answer?
Answers cannot be deleted once posted to maintain knowledge integrity. Ensure your answer is accurate before submitting.

### How does answer acceptance work?
The person who asked the question can accept one answer as the correct solution. Accepted answers earn bonus credits based on difficulty.

### Can I accept my own answer?
No, self-acceptance is not allowed to prevent credit farming.

### What is maintainer verification?
Maintainers are faculty or senior students who can verify the accuracy of answers. Verified answers receive a special badge and bonus credits.

## Platform Features

### How does the AI search work?
Sol-1 uses AI-powered search to find similar questions that have already been answered, reducing redundancy.

### Can I search for specific topics?
Yes, use the search bar with keywords, subject names, or question titles to find relevant content.

### Are there any mobile apps?
Currently, Sol-1 is web-based and mobile-responsive. Access it through your mobile browser.

## Technical Issues

### The platform is loading slowly. What should I do?
- Check your internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Report to support if the issue persists

### I found a bug. How do I report it?
Email support@iiitn.ac.in with:
- Description of the bug
- Steps to reproduce
- Screenshots if applicable
- Your browser and OS version

## Policies

### What content is not allowed?
- Plagiarized content
- Offensive or inappropriate material
- Spam or irrelevant posts
- Exam questions during active exams
- Personal attacks or harassment

### What happens if I violate the rules?
Violations may result in:
- Warning for first-time offenses
- Credit deduction for repeated violations
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent ban for serious violations

## Support

### How do I get help?
- Check these FAQs first

# IIIT Nagpur - Institute Information

## About IIIT Nagpur

**Indian Institute of Information Technology, Nagpur (IIITN)** is one of the 20 IIITs established under the **Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Scheme** by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

### Status
- **Institution of National Importance** under IIIT (PPP) Act, 2017
- Established: **2016**
- Permanent Campus: Butibori, Nagpur, Maharashtra

---

## Partners

### Government Partners
1. Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education, Government of India
2. Department of Higher Education, Government of Maharashtra (through Directorate of Technical Education)

### Industry Partners
1. **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)**, Mumbai
2. **Haldiram Foods International Pvt. Ltd.**, Nagpur

## Vision

To attain the status of a **top-notch institution in Information Technology** and emerge as an **elite research institution** by imparting futuristic quality education of global standards.

### Goals
- Achieve notable **NIRF ranking**
- Establish reputation in **research and innovation** for industrial and national needs
- Provide conducive environment for learning and research

---

## Mission

To undertake **socially relevant, industry-oriented** research & development programmes and cutting-edge research through public-private participation in Information Technology & allied fields.

Focus on developing **innovation and entrepreneurship** with social and environmental orientation.

## Strategic Initiatives

### 1. Permanent Campus Development
Fully functional permanent campus near Waranga, Nagpur providing supportive and enriching environment for quality learning & teaching.

### 2. Industry Interaction
- Strong links with industry for relevant teaching and research
- In-house IT Park for company campus presence
- Consultancy, sponsored research projects, student internships
- Joint workshops/seminars
- Enhanced CSR funding

### 3. Self-Sustainability
- Research grants and consultancy income
- Leveraging "IIIT" brand for industry support
- Alternate funding sources beyond government grants

### 4. Educational Expansion
- M.Tech programs in IT and allied fields
- "M.Tech through Research" for industry-sponsored candidates
- Certificate courses for special skills
- New undergraduate specializations

### 5. Internal Systems
- Comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) System
- Streamlined operations with improved efficiency and transparency
- Integrated finance management

### 6. International Collaboration
- Admission of international students
- Student exchange programmes with reputed universities abroad
- Building global alumni network

# About IIIT Nagpur

## Overview
Indian Institute of Information Technology, Nagpur (IIIT Nagpur) is a premier institution established by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The institute is committed to excellence in technical education and research.

## Academic Programs
IIIT Nagpur offers undergraduate (B.Tech) and postgraduate (M.Tech) programs in:
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Data Science and Artificial Intelligence

## Campus Life
The institute provides state-of-the-art infrastructure including:
- Modern classrooms with smart boards
- Well-equipped laboratories
- Central library with extensive digital resources
- Sports facilities and recreation areas
- Comfortable hostel accommodation

## Vision and Mission
**Vision**: To be a globally recognized institution for excellence in information technology education and research.

**Mission**: To nurture professionals with strong technical skills, ethical values, and innovative mindset to address societal challenges.

## Location
IIIT Nagpur is located in Nagpur, Maharashtra, known as the geographical center of India and a major IT hub in central India.

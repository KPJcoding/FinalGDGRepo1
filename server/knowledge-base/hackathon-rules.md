# Hackathon Rules and Guidelines

## Sol-1 Knowledge Hub Hackathon

### Registration
- Open only to IIIT Nagpur students with valid @iiitn.ac.in email
- Teams of 1-4 members allowed
- Registration deadline: As announced on the platform
- No registration fee required

### Submission Guidelines
1. **Question Submission**
   - Must be related to academic content
   - Include proper difficulty level (Easy/Medium/Hard)
   - Provide clear context and details
   - Avoid duplicate questions

2. **Answer Submission**
   - Provide detailed explanations
   - Include step-by-step solutions where applicable
   - Cite sources if using external references
   - Use proper formatting and code blocks

3. **Code of Conduct**
   - No plagiarism - all content must be original
   - Respect fellow participants
   - Maintain academic integrity
   - No spamming or abuse of the platform

### Scoring System
- **Question Asked**: Initial engagement credit
- **Answer Submitted**: Points for contribution
- **Answer Accepted**: Bonus credits based on difficulty
  - Easy: 10 credits
  - Medium: 25 credits
  - Hard: 50 credits
- **Peer Verification**: Additional bonus
- **Maintainer Verified**: Premium bonus (50 credits)

### Credit Tiers
- **Bronze**: 0-499 credits
- **Silver**: 500-1999 credits
- **Gold**: 2000-4999 credits
- **Platinum**: 5000+ credits

### Prohibited Activities
- Creating multiple accounts
- Accepting own answers
- Vote manipulation
- Copying answers from external sources without attribution
- Posting irrelevant or offensive content

### Prizes and Recognition
- Top contributors will be recognized on the leaderboard
- Special badges for verified contributors
- Certificate of participation for active users
- Prizes for hackathon winners (to be announced)

### Timeline
- Hackathon runs throughout the semester
- Weekly leaderboard updates
- Monthly recognition of top contributors

For any queries or issues:

## Placement Policy Summary

### Eligibility
- Must be registered with T&P Office
- Company-specific eligibility criteria apply
- CGPA criteria is strictly enforced

### One Student One Job Policy
- Students get placed in first accepted offer
- Can upgrade **only twice** if:
  - New offer is **1.5x** current package
  - Overall placement rate ≥ 50%

### CGPA-Based Skip Policy

| CGPA Range | Max Companies to Skip |
|------------|----------------------|
| 5.0 - 7.0  | 2 times              |
| 7.0 - 8.0  | 3 times              |
| 8.0 - 9.0  | 4 times              |
| Above 9.0  | 5 times              |

### Important Rules
- **No withdrawals** after application deadline
- **Dress code**: Business formals mandatory
- Must carry: ID card, resume (2 copies), mark sheets, certificates
- Late arrivals not allowed
- Off-campus offers must be reported immediately

---

## Internship Policy

### Key Points
- **One Student One Internship** policy
- Once accepted, cannot apply to other companies
- Two internships allowed in same semester if:
  - Both in same field/role
  - Not simultaneous

### Pre-Placement Offers (PPO)
- Must inform T&P Office immediately
- If no response in 7 days = deemed accepted
- Student out of placement process

# Sol-1 Platform Information

## About Sol-1

Sol-1 is the official knowledge-sharing platform for IIIT Nagpur students. It serves as a comprehensive hub for academic questions, verified solutions, and community interaction.

## Platform Statistics

- **2,847** Verified Solutions
- **1,234** Active Contributors
- **98.2%** Accuracy Rate
- **6** Semesters Covered

These statistics represent the collective knowledge and contributions of the IIIT Nagpur student community.

## Platform Goals

- Build a permanent knowledge repository for IIIT Nagpur
- Help students find verified solutions to academic questions
- Foster a collaborative learning community
- Recognize and reward active contributors
- Connect students through clubs and activities
- Maintain high-quality, verified content

## Access

Sol-1 is exclusively for IIIT Nagpur students. Access requires a valid @iiitn.ac.in email address.

## Technology

The platform uses advanced AI technology to:
- Match questions with relevant existing solutions
- Provide intelligent search capabilities
- Offer an interactive chatbot for quick answers
- Organize and categorize content effectively
